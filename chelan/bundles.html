Step One: Pick Your Perfect Fit

Thinking about listing your business, organization, and/or events in this directory? We’d love to have you aboard!

  • Adding or claiming a listing is easy, quick, and FREE — no kidding
  • Listings must be based in Chelan, WA

Why Jump In?

This site is downtown Chelan’s online directory — the handy little tool locals and visitors use to find local businesses, nonprofits, organizations, and events, all in one place. We actively promote this website and our featured businesses and sponsors in the community through seasonal programs, social media, and more. Listings on our site are discoverable on search engines like Google.
