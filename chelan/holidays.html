Small Town Holidays in Historic Downtown Chelan

November 27 – December 2026

The holiday magic is in full swing in Historic Downtown Chelan! On Friday, November 27th, the Historic Downtown Chelan Association kicks off Small Town Holidays with Plaid Friday shopping all day, caroling at the Christmas tree, the Santa Parade, and the Christmas Tree Lighting. Bring the family downtown and make a day of it.

The Christmas tree stands at the corner of Woodin Avenue and Emerson Street (203 E Woodin Ave), in the heart of downtown. The season rolls on with Shop Small Saturday on November 28 and the Holiday Wish List at downtown shops through December.

How Friday the 27th Runs

  1. All dayPlaid Friday deals at downtown shops
  2. 5:00 PMCaroling & mini concert at the Christmas tree
  3. 6:00 PMSanta Parade down Woodin Avenue
  4. 6:00 PMChristmas Tree Lighting at Woodin & Emerson
  5. Sat, Nov 28Shop Small Saturday, plus a free Arthur Christmas matinee at the Ruby Theatre at 3 PM

Plaid Friday & Shop Small Saturday

Plaid Friday (November 27) and Shop Small Saturday (November 28) are a wonderful opportunity to support the locally owned businesses that make Historic Downtown Chelan special. From unique gifts and holiday decor to delicious food and one-of-a-kind treasures, downtown has something for everyone on your Christmas list. Every purchase helps support our local businesses and keeps our downtown community thriving.

Friday & Saturday, November 27–28 Historic Downtown Chelan Free, family friendly

The Holiday Wish List

All season long, downtown shops keep Holiday Wish Lists in store — and 20 downtown merchants take part. Look for the North Pole Outpost cling in shop windows around downtown.

How the Wish List Works

1

Stop by any of the 20 participating downtown merchants.

2

Make your Holiday Wish List in store — the gifts you would love to find under the tree.

3

Share it with family and friends — they shop your list downtown, and every purchase supports local businesses.

Santa Parade & the Tree Lighting

Santa Claus is coming to town — and he’s got a big evening planned. Here is how it runs:

  • 5:00 PM: Caroling and a mini concert at the Christmas tree, on the corner of Woodin Avenue and Emerson Street (203 E Woodin Ave).
  • 6:00 PM: The Santa Parade rolls down Woodin Avenue — line the sidewalks and cheer him in.
  • 6:00 PM: The Christmas Tree Lighting — Santa lights the downtown Christmas tree and officially opens the Small Town Holidays season in Chelan.
  • All day: Plaid Friday deals at downtown shops, so come early and shop before the show.

Small Town Holidays, All Season

Small Town Holidays runs from the November 27 kick-off through December, with the Holiday Wish List at 20 downtown merchants, Plaid Friday and Shop Small Saturday deals, and downtown dressed for the season.
