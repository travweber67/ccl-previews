How Small Town Holidays Works

Friday, November 27 is the biggest day of the season downtown. Shop Plaid Friday deals, gather at the tree for caroling, and stick around for Santa, the parade, and the tree lighting.

  1. Shop downtown. Plaid Friday deals run at downtown shops all day Friday, November 27, and Shop Small Saturday follows on November 28.
  2. Gather at the tree. Caroling and a mini concert at the Christmas tree, corner of Woodin Ave and Emerson St, start at 5 PM.
  3. Watch Santa arrive. The Santa Parade rolls down Woodin Avenue at 6 PM.
  4. Stay for the lights. Santa lights the downtown Christmas tree at 6 PM, right after the parade.

All season: the Holiday Wish List at 20 downtown merchants, and a free Arthur Christmas matinee at the Ruby Theatre on Saturday, November 28 at 3 PM.

Full details are on the Holidays page and at historicchelan.org.

About Your Downtown Crew

The Historic Downtown Chelan Association (HDCA) is part of the national Main Street America network and a Washington Main Street community, on a mission to preserve the history and enhance the future of Chelan’s downtown core. We strengthen local business, encourage new business, and connect the community through partnerships — showing off local businesses here in the directory, on social media, and through seasonal programs like Small Town Holidays, and helping visitors find great places to stay, eat, shop, and visit.
