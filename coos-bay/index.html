Shop Local for the Holidays

Downtown Coos Bay’s holiday season is on. Shop Small Saturday and Santa Comes to Town lands Saturday, November 28, with the Downtown Cider Walk, visits from Santa, the Lighted Walking Parade, and the annual Christmas Tree Lighting.

How Shop Small Saturday Works

Saturday, November 28 is the big day downtown. Shop local, save your receipts, and stay for Santa, the parade, and the tree lighting.

  1. Shop downtown. Spend at participating downtown businesses and restaurants between November 13 and 28, and save your receipts.
  2. Redeem your stein. Receipts totaling $100 or more earn a commemorative stein at Jennie’s Shoes on November 28, 11 AM to 3 PM, during the Downtown Cider Walk.
  3. See Santa. Santa visits downtown businesses 1:30 to 3 PM, then takes photos at the Coos Bay Downtown Association office from 3 to 5 PM.
  4. Stay for the lights. The Lighted Walking Parade gathers at 5:15 PM, and Santa and Mayor Joe Benetti light the downtown Christmas tree at about 5:45 PM.

All day: warm cider, holiday shopping, and one-of-a-kind finds at downtown businesses.

Full details are on the Holidays page and in the News post from the Coos Bay Downtown Association.

About Us

The Coos Bay Downtown Association is part of the national Main Street Program. We work to foster the development of downtown Coos Bay by strengthening local business, encouraging new business, and connecting the community through partnerships. We show off local businesses here on our online directory, on social media, and through our seasonal programs, and we help visitors find great places to stay, eat, shop, or visit from our members.
