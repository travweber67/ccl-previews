Start at the Visitor Information Center

New to the West End? Stop by the Forks Visitor Information Center at 1411 S Forks Ave, run by the Forks Chamber of Commerce. Pick up lodging and dining guides, maps for the Hoh Rainforest and the La Push and Rialto beaches, fishing information, and a self-guided map of Twilight locations around town.

Plan Your Visit

Forks sits on US-101 on the west side of the Olympic Peninsula — timber country turned visitor hub. Stay in town for the rainforest and beaches, fish the Sol Duc, Bogachiel and Calawah rivers, and see the Forks locations Stephenie Meyer wrote into the Twilight books.

Shop. Taste. Play. Choose Local First

Between the rainforest valleys of the Hoh, Bogachiel and Sol Duc rivers and the wild beaches at La Push and Rialto, Forks and its West End neighbors have a lot to offer. Come see for yourself.

About Us

The Forks Chamber of Commerce promotes Forks and the West End of the Olympic Peninsula. We run the Visitor Information Center, publish lodging and dining guides, and connect visitors with the local businesses, outfitters and organizations listed in this directory.
