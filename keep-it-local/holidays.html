The Wonderland Card

Experience the thrill of local shopping with the Wonderland Card! With your card in hand, shop local at any of our 100+ participating locations to earn a stamp for every $10 you spend. Once your card(s) are complete, turn them in for a chance to win $5000 and 30+ incredible prizes! Plus, there will be a $150 cash prize winner in each city! This is a fun way to support small businesses, AND, you might just land a big holiday win!

$5,000 grand prize $150 winner in each city Winners drawn live Jan 15

A list of participating locations is below ↓

How the Wonderland Card Works

1

For every $10* you spend at a participating business, an employee will stamp off one box on your card.

2

When all 20 boxes are full, sign and take your completed card(s) to one of the drop-off card locations

3

To enter the grand prize drawing!

One visit per day at financial institutions and service-type businesses is equal to one box. Credit Unions may give 2 stamps for new opened accounts and a full card for new loans.

When is the drawing?

The winners will be drawn live on Keep It Local Columbia County’s Facebook page January 15th — and notified afterwards. You do not have to watch the live video to win.

Can I turn in multiple incomplete cards?

So long as you have at least 20 stamps between the cards, we will count your entry! Staple them together and drop them off at a Wonderland Card drop off location to enter the grand drawing.

Additional Terms & Conditions

  • Submitting a Wonderland Card means the participant agrees to these terms and conditions.
  • There is a 50 card limit per person for the drawing.
  • Winning any prize (including the $5000 cash prize, $150/city prizes, or the 30+ donated prizes) is not guaranteed by your participation.
  • No purchase is necessary for stamps collected by visiting local credit unions, the Clatskanie PUD, and realtor offices. Up to 20 Wonderland Cards can be submitted this way. An additional 30 Wonderland Cards can be earned without a purchase by completing 50 locations on the Bigfoot Trail. More information on the Trail is below.
  • Only 18 years+ participants are eligible to win the $5000 cash prize.
  • All ages can win the 30+ donated prizes and $150/city cash prizes. Participants under 18 must have parent or legal guardian consent to enter these drawings. If a minor wins, the City of Scappoose (which hosts Keep it Local) will coordinate with their parent or legal guardian to claim the prize, subject to the City’s approved procedures.

The Bigfoot Trail

From November 13th to January 8th, Bigfoot is shopping local across Columbia County’s Wonderland, leaving a trail of footprints behind in 90+ businesses! Each footprint has a unique QR code. Find and scan 7 codes using the free “Columbia County” mobile app to earn a FREE collectible ornament* and a chance to win $500!

Don’t forget to bring your Wonderland Card along. Happy searching! *While supplies last. We have 100+ ornaments to give away!

How to Scan a Bigfoot Footprint

  1. Download the FREE “Columbia County Oregon” mobile app on Apple or Google Play here: linktr.ee/keepitlocalcc
  2. Open the app and log in or create an account.
  3. iPhone: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Columbia County, OR App. Choose “While Using the App” and turn on “Precise Location.”
  4. Android: Go to Settings > Apps > Columbia County, OR app > Permissions > Location. Choose “Allow only while using the app” and turn on “Use precise location.”
  5. Ensure you set up your app to share your location. This is required to ensure participants are at the place of business when they check-in.
  6. Tap the green “Trails” button on the home screen, then select “Bigfoot Trail.”
  7. Visit a participating business and find Bigfoot’s hidden footprint in that business.
  8. Once you find a footprint, find and click the listing of that business under the app’s Bigfoot Trail. Then tap the “Scan QR Code” button in that business’s listing to scan the QR code on Bigfoot’s footprint.
  9. Repeat at 6 more locations! Once you’ve completed 7 locations, you’re entered into the $500 drawing and can redeem your FREE collectible ornament!

Ornament Redeeming Locations

After completing 7 locations on the Bigfoot Trail, click the “submit” button at the top to complete the Trail and fill out the form. The cover will then turn green. After that, visit any of the locations below to claim your ornament by showing a staff member your completion screen.

  • Clatskanie, Vernonia, Scappoose: Wauna Credit Union
  • St. Helens: Sass at Home, Richardson’s Furniture & Mattress Gallery, Crooked Creek Brewery, Wauna Credit Union
  • Rainier: Rainier City Hall

Bonus Challenge

Feeling like a Bigfoot sighting champion? Then this challenge is for you! Visit 50 Bigfoot Trail locations to earn 30 filled-out Wonderland Cards for even more chances to win holiday prizes, including our $5,000 grand prize!

Additional Terms

  • All ages can participate in the Bigfoot footprint hunt and receive an ornament. Participants under 18 must have parent or legal guardian consent to enter the cash prize drawing. If a minor wins, the City of Scappoose (which hosts Keep it Local) will coordinate with their parent or legal guardian to claim the prize, subject to the City’s approved procedures.
  • No purchase is necessary to find footprints, collect an ornament, or enter the $500 drawing. Winning is not guaranteed by your participation.

Give the Gift of Local with the

Columbia County E-Gift Card

How It Works

Purchase an e-gift card online HERE and send it to your friends, family, employees, or coworkers via email or text. They can then enjoy spending it year-round at 30+ local shops, restaurants, and services. It’s the perfect gift that offers variety, supports local, and makes a difference.

The card runs on Yiftee. Buying one adds a $1 + 5% eDelivery fee on top of the card’s face value — and participating businesses pay nothing extra to accept it: they receive the full face value, minus only their normal card-processing fee.

For local businesses, the E-Gift Card is part of the bundles: E-Gift Card participation comes with the Business and Sponsor bundles, and sponsoring the E-Gift Card is a Sponsor-tier perk. See the bundles →
