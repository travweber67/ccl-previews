The Bigfoot Trail

Bigfoot is exploring Columbia County’s Wonderland, and he has left a trail of footprints behind in 70+ local businesses. Track them down with the free app and collect them as you shop.

  1. Get the app. Download the free “Columbia County, OR” app and open the Bigfoot Trail.
  2. Find a footprint. Look for the wooden footprint sign hanging in participating businesses. It says “You Found One!”
  3. Scan to check in. Scan the footprint’s QR code with the app’s Bigfoot Trail feature to collect it.
  4. Collect 7. Find 7 footprints to earn a free collectible ornament and an entry into the $500 cash prize drawing.

Bonus challenge: visit 50 trail locations and earn 30 filled Wonderland Cards.

The trail runs in the app from Nov 13 to Jan 8, alongside the Wonderland Card. Every stop is a local business taking part in Keep it Local for the Holidays.

About Us

We believe that supporting local helps communities flourish. With this at the heart of what we do, we are a small business marketing nonprofit that bolsters Columbia County Oregon's economy by persuading residents and visitors to shop, taste, play, and choose local first. To do so, we show off local businesses here on our online directory, on social media, and through our seasonal programs.
