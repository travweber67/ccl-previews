About Us

We believe that supporting local helps communities flourish. With this at the heart of what we do, we are a small business marketing nonprofit that bolsters Columbia County Oregon's economy by persuading residents and visitors to shop, taste, play, and choose local first. To do so, we show off local businesses here on our online directory, on social media, and through our seasonal programs.

Meet Our Incredible Sponsors

Our program is, in large part, made possible because of our incredible sponsors. These are the organizations that invested in our small business community's success, advocate for supporting local, and love Columbia County. A huge thanks to our sponsors for being so supportive of our program and community, we appreciate you! Sponsors include Wauna Credit Union, InRoads Credit Union, Columbia River PUD, Clatskanie PUD, Richardson's Furniture, Sass at Home, and Crooked Creek Brewery.
