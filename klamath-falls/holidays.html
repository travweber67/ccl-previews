Holidays in Downtown Klamath Falls

November 28 – December 21, 2026

The holiday magic is in full swing in Downtown Klamath Falls! The season opens with the Klamath Snowflake Festival on November 28, the downtown Christmas tree lights up at Klamath Commons on Monday, November 30, and the Hometown Holiday Window Contest keeps Main Street glowing through December 21.

Downtown businesses register their holiday windows November 1 through December 1, building the festive, snowy atmosphere the Merry and Bright Tour is all about.

How the Season Runs

  1. Nov 1 – Dec 1Window contest registration for downtown businesses
  2. Sat, Nov 28Klamath Snowflake Festival opens, running through Dec 13
  3. Mon, Nov 30 · 5:30 PMHometown Christmas Tree Lighting at Klamath Commons
  4. Dec 7 – 18Merry and Bright Tour & window contest voting
  5. Thu, Dec 10 · 7 PMSnowflake Parade down Main Street
  6. Mon, Dec 21Window contest winner announced on Facebook

The Hometown Christmas Tree Lighting

On Monday, November 30, from 5:30 to 7:00 PM, the community gathers at Klamath Commons, on the corner of 11th and Main, to light the downtown Christmas tree. It is the Klamath Falls Downtown Association’s holiday centerpiece: one big tree, one hometown crowd, and the official start of the season downtown.

Monday, November 30 5:30 – 7:00 PM Klamath Commons, 11th & Main Free, family friendly

Hometown Holiday Window Contest

Help us create a fun and festive snowy atmosphere throughout Downtown Klamath Falls! Participating businesses bring the community downtown to enjoy the holiday displays, explore local shopping, food, and services, and experience the fun and beauty downtown.

How the Contest Works

1

Register: downtown businesses register their hometown holiday window between November 1 and December 1.

2

Decorate: windows go up across downtown, each one a stop on the Merry and Bright Tour.

3

Vote: take a stroll downtown December 7 through 18, enjoy the fantastic holiday displays, and cast your vote for your favorite.

Voting concludes the week of December 21, when the winner is announced on the Association’s Facebook page.

Klamath Snowflake Festival & the Snowflake Parade

The Klamath Snowflake Festival runs November 28 through December 13, 2026, with holiday events at the Running Y Resort and venues across Klamath Falls. Its biggest night downtown is the Snowflake Parade: Thursday, December 10 at 7:00 PM down Main Street. This year’s theme is “There’s Snow Place Like Home.” Parade entries were due December 2.

November 28 – December 13 Parade: Thu, Dec 10, 7 PM Main Street, Downtown

Shop Local for the Holidays

Most of our downtown businesses are open Monday through Saturday, 10 AM to 5 PM. From unique gifts and holiday decor to delicious food and one-of-a-kind treasures, downtown has something for everyone on your Christmas list. Every purchase helps support our local businesses and keeps our downtown community thriving.
