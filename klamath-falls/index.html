How the Hometown Holiday Window Contest Works

Downtown businesses dress their windows for the season, and the whole community picks the winner.

  1. Register. Downtown businesses register their hometown holiday window with the Association between November 1 and December 1.
  2. Decorate. Windows go up all over downtown, creating a fun, festive, snowy atmosphere on Main Street.
  3. Take the tour. From December 7 to 18, stroll the Merry and Bright Tour, enjoy the holiday displays, and cast your vote for your favorite.
  4. Winner announced. The winning window is announced December 21 on the Association’s Facebook page.

All season: holiday shopping, food, and services at downtown businesses, lit windows included.

Full details are on the Holidays page and the Association’s Facebook page.

About Your Downtown Crew

The Klamath Falls Downtown Association is an accredited Main Street America program, dedicated to serving the business community of our area. We strengthen local business, encourage new business, and connect the community through partnerships. We show off local businesses here on our online directory, on social media, and through our seasonal programs, and we help visitors find great places to stay, eat, shop, or visit from our members.
