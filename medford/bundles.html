Step One: Pick Your Level

Thinking about listing your business, organization, and/or events in this directory? We’d love to have you aboard!

  • Adding a listing is easy and quick, and Friends of Downtown starts at just $60 a year
  • Listings must be based in downtown Medford, OR

Why Jump In?

This site is downtown Medford’s online directory, the tool locals and visitors use to find local businesses, nonprofits, organizations, and events, all in one place. The Association promotes this website and its partners through Third Fridays, seasonal programs, social media, and more. Listings on our site are discoverable on search engines like Google.
