Holidays in Downtown Medford

November 2026 to February 2027

The holiday magic is in full swing in Downtown Medford! On Saturday, December 5th, the City of Medford’s Winter Lights Festival takes over Pear Blossom Park from 5 to 8 PM, with the tree lighting, an illuminated parade, live performances, food trucks and photos with Santa.

All season long, the Downtown Medford Association’s Holiday Lights program wraps the Main Street trees in thousands of lights, from Bear Creek Bridge to Fir Street, mid-November through the end of February. Each tree costs $180 to light, and the Downtown Medford Foundation is raising funds now.

Shop Local for the Holidays

The holidays are a wonderful opportunity to support the locally owned businesses that make Downtown Medford special. From unique gifts and holiday decor to delicious food and one-of-a-kind treasures, downtown has something for everyone on your Christmas list. Every purchase helps support our local businesses and keeps our downtown community thriving.

Saturday, December 5 Pear Blossom Park Free, family friendly

Holiday Lights: Light Up Downtown Medford

The Association’s Holiday Lights program transforms Main Street and Vogel Plaza with thousands of lights each season. The lit trees line Main Street from Bear Creek Bridge to Fir Street, installed by Mary & Brite’s, and stay up from mid-November through the end of February.

How Sponsoring a Tree Works

1

Pick a gift level. Each tree costs $180 to light for the season.

2

Give through the Downtown Medford Foundation’s Holiday Lights fundraiser.

3

Enjoy the glow: the lights go up in mid-November and shine through February.

Santa, the Parade, and the Tree Lighting

Santa Claus is coming to Pear Blossom Park. Here is how the festival runs:

  • 5:00 PM: The festival opens at Pear Blossom Park, 204 N Bartlett St, with live performances, vendor booths and food trucks.
  • Photos with Santa: Bring your cameras; Santa poses for photos during the festival.
  • The illuminated parade: The parade sets off from the Children’s Museum’s Fir Street location and joins the festival.
  • The tree lighting: The evening’s centerpiece, lighting the big tree at Pear Blossom Park and officially welcoming the Christmas season to Medford.

Third Friday, Holiday Edition

Downtown’s holiday season kicks off with Third Friday on November 20, 2026, when galleries and shops stay open late with receptions, extended hours and seasonal specials, and again on December 18.
