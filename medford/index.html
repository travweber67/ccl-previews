How the Winter Lights Festival Works

Saturday, December 5 is the biggest night of the season downtown. The City of Medford’s Winter Lights Festival takes over Pear Blossom Park from 5 to 8 PM, and the Association’s Holiday Lights glow on Main Street all season.

  1. Head to Pear Blossom Park. The festival runs 5 to 8 PM at 204 N Bartlett St, with live performances, vendor booths and food trucks.
  2. Watch the tree light up. The tree lighting is the centerpiece of the evening, and the illuminated parade sets off from the Children’s Museum’s Fir Street location.
  3. See Santa. Photos with Santa are part of the festival all evening.
  4. Stay for the lights. After the festival, stroll Main Street from Bear Creek Bridge to Fir Street, where the Association’s Holiday Lights canopy glows mid-November through February.

All season: each sponsored tree costs $180, and the Downtown Medford Foundation is raising funds now to light up Main Street and Vogel Plaza.

Full details are on the Holidays page and in the News posts from the Downtown Medford Association.

About Your Downtown Crew

The Downtown Medford Association exists to provide inspired leadership and advocacy that encourages engagement to enhance the physical, cultural, and economic vitality of our downtown. Formed in 2018 and built on the Main Street Four-Point Approach, the Association runs Third Fridays, the Holiday Lights, and this directory, and in 2026 downtown Medford advanced to a Designated Oregon Main Street community.
