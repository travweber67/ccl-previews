About Us

David and Tiffany Kallio, aka Count and Countess O'illak, are the founders and owners of Northwest Saber Syndicate (NWSS). Based out of Saint Helens, Oregon, USA aka Halloween Town.

What started off as an opportunity to fulfill his childhood dream, quickly developed in to a passion and determination to get a lightsaber in everyone's home.

Northwest Saber Syndicate is a proud vendor of Nexus Sabers formerly known as LGT Saber Studios.

We are dedicated to providing the best customer service, quick response time, and guarantee a friendly interaction 100% of the time!
