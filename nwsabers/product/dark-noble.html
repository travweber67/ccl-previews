The Cerean is a variant version of Ki-Adi-Mundi's lightsaber. He used it during many campaigns in the Clone Wars against the Separatist Droid Army including the first and the second battles of Geonosis. Mundi continued using his lightsaber until his death during the Fourth Battle of Mygeeto. This models design has the heat-coils under the emitter, which gives great two-handed wielding control for its users. This model comes with a Covertec Wheel attached to the lightsaber, reach out to us if you would like to purchase a Covertec belt clip ($20), if you are interested in hanging the lightsaber from your belt for cosplay, Halloween, etc.   Hilt Length: 10.43 inches (26.5 cm)
