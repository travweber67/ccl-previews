Home / Shop / Pixel to Base-Lit Adapter

The Pixel to Base-Lit Adapter allows the user to convert their pixel blade lightsaber into an RGB base-lit blade lightsaber, yet still using their pixel electronics. It is placed into the emitter and is held in place by your base-lit blade. The adapter features the CREE XP-E2 LED which is the brightest RGB base-lit LED on the market, providing all the colors of the rainbow.    (Pixel to Base-Lit Adapter are designed to be used with LGT Saber Studio and Nexus Sabers only)   *Lockable version uses .7 inch of the emitter depth (used with the character lightsabers) *Non-Lockable/Drop-in version uses .55 inch of the emitter depth (used with the non-character lightsabers)   *If the pixel lightsaber you are placing the Adapter into does not have more than 1 inch of depth to secure the blade into, it may not be a good option to purchase this Adapter, because it uses over a half inch of emitter depth space, which only leaves less than a half inch to secure the blade into place, which is not a preferred method, if you plan on dueling with the lightsaber.
