Home / Shop / Quick Connect Coupler

From the Nexus Sabers parts and accessories range at NW Saber Syndicate.

The Quick Connect Couplers are designed to allow the user to quickly connect or disconnect two non-character lightsabers, which provides a quick way to go from a double-bladed staff lightsaber to being two lightsabers.

To connect two lightsabers together, push the textured grip section into the coupler while pulling down on the middle spring section.

To disconnect two lightsabers from each other, pull down on the textured grip section.
