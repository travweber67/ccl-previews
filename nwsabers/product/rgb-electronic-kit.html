Home / Shop / Electronic Core Kits

Electronic Core Kits come with emitter, electronics, speaker, and charge port/activation switch, all enclosed in a chassis. If you would like to replace or upgrade your electronics, you have come to the correct place!    Electronic cores feature either a round charge port or a USB-C charge port. USB-C charge port will fit all non-character hilts, however, the round charge port will only fit round charge port non-character hilts. Make sure to specify which you need, round or USB-C, during checkout in the mandatory comment field. If we are out of stock of the round charge port electronic core kits, you will receive a USB-C electronic core kit.   IMPORTANT: The cores are available for non-character lightsaber hilts, if you purchased a character lightsaber hilt, please reach out prior to purchasing and we can help make sure you get the correct core, if we have it available in-stock.   *If you are upgrading to a Xeno3-pixel electronic core then make sure to purchase a pixel blade, because the Xeno3-pixel electronics require a pixel blade to provide all of the special blade effects.

It's recommended to charge your lightsaber using only the 5V1A power cord included with your purchase, and only plugged in to a surge-protected power strip or slow-powering electronic device.
