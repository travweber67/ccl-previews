The Scavenger, Rey, possibly a Skywalker, or a Palpatine, for now, it is unknown, however this model was pieced together from Rey's staff she wielded prior to learning how-to use the Force. Rey, was a human female Jedi Master who fought on the side of the Resistance in the war against the First Order. A former scavenger from the planet Jakku, her life was changed by the tumultuous events of the last days of the New Republic Era.     This model is designed to be a one-handed lightsaber, and is VERY comfortable in the hands of the user.  I did not think I would like this lightsaber, until I had it in my hands. Now, it is one of my favorite one-handed lightsabers for backyard battles, cosplay, and is display worthy!   Hilt Length: 10.63 inches (32 cm)
