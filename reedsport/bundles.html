Support Main Street

Sponsorships, Sized for Small Business

Step One: Pick Your Level

The Reedsport Main Street Program offers flexible sponsorship levels so businesses can participate in a way that fits their needs. Every level supports the events and programs that keep Reedsport's downtown, midtown and uptown main streets thriving, from the Pop-Up Vendor Markets to the Christmas Tree Lighting.

  • Listings and sponsorships are for businesses and organizations in Reedsport, OR
  • Supporter sponsors are included in this Main Street business directory

Why Jump In?

This site is Reedsport's online directory: the tool locals and visitors use to find local businesses, nonprofits, organizations, and events, all in one place. The program promotes this directory and its sponsors through seasonal programs, events, and social media all year.

No account. No login. No fuss. In the live directory, this button opens a short form: your business name, address, phone, website, and a photo. It only takes a couple of minutes, and the program follows up about your sponsorship level.
