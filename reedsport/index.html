The Deck the Halls light contest runs all season, and Moonlight Madness lights up local shops on December 5. Grab the family and come make a day of it.

How the Holiday Weekend Works

Saturday, November 28 is the biggest day of the season in Reedsport. Shop the bazaar, pick out a tree, and stick around for the tree lighting at Lions Park.

  1. Start at the bazaar. The Reedsport Holiday Bazaar fills the Community Center from 10 AM to 4 PM, all small and local vendors.
  2. Pick out a tree. The Lions Club Christmas tree lot opens the same day in the McKay’s Market parking lot on Highway 101.
  3. Light the tree. At 5 PM, the whole town gathers at Lions Park, 150 N 20th Street, for the annual Christmas Tree Lighting.
  4. Keep the season going. The Deck the Halls holiday light contest runs through December, and Moonlight Madness brings late-night shopping on Saturday, December 5, 5 to 9 PM.

All day: holiday shopping and one-of-a-kind finds at Reedsport businesses. Rain jacket optional, good cheer guaranteed.

About Your Main Street Crew

The Reedsport Main Street Program is a City of Reedsport program in the Oregon Main Street Network, and our mission is to promote the economic, social, cultural and historic value of Reedsport. Our vision: a unified and vibrant place to live, work, shop and play across our downtown, midtown and uptown main streets. We show off local businesses here in our online directory, on social media, and through our seasonal programs, from the Pop-Up Vendor Markets to the Christmas Tree Lighting.
