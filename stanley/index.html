Visit Stanley & the Sawtooths

Stanley sits at 6,250 feet where Highways 21 and 75 meet, at the headwaters of the Salmon River and the edge of the Sawtooth Wilderness. Fewer than 200 people live here year-round — and the Sawtooth National Recreation Area around town covers 756,000 acres of peaks, lakes, and river.

6,250 fttown elevation
756,000acres of Sawtooth NRA
300+alpine lakes nearby
40 miof Salmon River headwaters

Summer brings rafting, fishing, hiking, and horseback rides. Winter brings some of the coldest, clearest days in the Lower 48 — plus snowmobiling, Nordic skiing, the outdoor ice rink, Winterfest, and the Sawtooth Outdoor Bonspiel.

About Us

The Stanley-Sawtooth Chamber of Commerce supports the small businesses of the Sawtooth Valley — Stanley, Lower Stanley, and the Redfish Lake and Galena areas. We show off local businesses here in our online directory and through community events like Winterfest, so residents and visitors alike can shop, taste, play, and choose local first.
