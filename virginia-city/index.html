Visiting Virginia City

Virginia City grew up around the Comstock Lode, the great silver strike of 1859 that made it one of the richest mining towns in the West. Today the whole town is a National Historic Landmark district — a living ghost town where you can walk the C Street boardwalks, ride the V&T Railroad, tour mansions and museums, and hear live music in saloons that have poured drinks since the 1860s and 1870s.

About Us

The Virginia City Tourism Commission is Storey County's tourism board, promoting Virginia City, Nevada — the Comstock Lode boomtown that became one of the largest National Historic Landmark districts in the United States. This directory gathers the town's shops, saloons, restaurants, museums, lodging and community organizations in one searchable place.
