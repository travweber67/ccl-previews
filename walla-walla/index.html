How the Holidays Work Downtown

The season builds from Shop Small Saturday on November 28 to the biggest night of the year downtown: Saturday, December 5, with the tree lighting at 5 PM and the Holiday Parade of Lights at 6 PM.

  1. Shop small first. Spend Saturday, November 28 downtown for Shop Small Saturday, 11 AM to 5 PM, and show the small businesses some extra love.
  2. Taste the season. Holiday Barrel Weekend runs December 4 to 6, with barrel tastings and new vintages at wineries and downtown tasting rooms across the valley.
  3. Light the tree. The community tree lighting takes place at 5 PM on Saturday, December 5 at Walawala Plaza, First Avenue and Main Street.
  4. Stay for the parade. The Holiday Parade of Lights steps off at 6 PM from Alder and Fifth, with more than 100 lighted entries and Santa bringing up the rear. The parade is sponsored by Nelson Construction.

All season: holiday shopping, tasting rooms pouring new vintages, and one-of-a-kind finds at downtown businesses.

Full details are on the Holidays page and in the announcements from the Downtown Walla Walla Foundation.

About Your Downtown Crew

The Downtown Walla Walla Foundation is Walla Walla’s Main Street organization, and we’re on a mission: help downtown Walla Walla shine. We strengthen local business, encourage new business, and connect the community through partnerships. We show off local businesses here on our online directory, on social media, and through our seasonal programs, and we help visitors find great places to stay, eat, shop, taste, or visit from our members.
